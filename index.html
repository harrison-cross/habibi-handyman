Good to know

Common questions.

Can I enquire about a small job?

Yes. Send a message about what you need, whether it’s one repair or a short list of jobs.

What should I include when requesting a quote?

Include your suburb, a short description of the job and a few clear photos if you have them. If you have a date in mind, mention that too.

How do I check whether you service my area?

Send your suburb through Facebook along with the job details so Habibi Handyman can confirm whether they can help.
